90 min · Wall + hangboard

Warm-up - 15 min

5 min easy traverse · joint circles · 3-4 easy boulders building intensity

Hangboard - 15 min

3 sets × 6 reps of (10s hang / 5s rest), open hand then half crimp · rest 2-3 min between sets · edge where the 10s hang is RPE 7-8. Skip or halve volume if fingers feel tweaky.

Bouldering - 45 min

Technique-focused projecting on 2-4 boulders near your limit. Movement quality over sending.

2×10

2×12
Progression: smaller edge or added load every 2-3 weeks, not every session - finger connective tissue adapts far slower than muscle.

Sunday - Easy Movement / Light Climb

60 min · Before your 3-8p front desk shift

Warm-up - 10 min

Easy joint mobility

Main - 30 min

ARCing / easy traversing well below your limit - continuous, low-intensity movement. RPE 3-4, nothing that taxes fingers or forearms.

Mobility / stretch - 15 min

Full-body light stretch, easy breathing

Keep it genuinely easy: you're heading into your front desk shift right after this. The point is to leave you fresher, not more fatigued.

Watch this

Mixing heavy lifting with hangboarding in the same week is normal for climbers, but it's also the single most common way climbers develop elbow and finger overuse injuries (golfer's/tennis elbow, pulley strain). Two rules worth keeping without exception: never hangboard on a day where fingers/forearms are already fatigued from the day before, and if any finger or elbow discomfort lingers more than a day, drop the hangboard block entirely - swap in extra bouldering volume or rest instead. Push through muscle fatigue if you want; don't push through joint pain.
Why the split looks like this: strength days (Mon/Fri) land on your two longest academic-plus-workout days because you have the biggest gym windows after class. Climbing days (Wed/Sat) split power (Wed, class-adjacent) from volume (Sat, no class to rush to). Tue/Thu stay low-intensity on purpose - they're your two heaviest class days, and their job is to protect your joints, not add training stress. Sunday stays light since your BKB shift starts right after. This is a starting template - update sets/reps as you progress, and swap a strength day for extra recovery if a hard week of cold-calls has you running on empty.
