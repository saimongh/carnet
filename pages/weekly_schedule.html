Why it's routed this way

home→gym (50m) + gym→school (40m) = 1h30, which beats going straight home→school (1h40). So on every class day, the gym sits on the way between school and home instead of costing a separate trip - you're not spending extra commute time to work out, you're saving 10 minutes over not working out.
Tue/Thu mornings are the hidden asset: no class before 1:50pm means 2-3.5 hours of at-home deep work before you ever have to commute - Thursday's 3h35 morning block is the single biggest study window in the week.
You're logging ~17.7 hours/week in transit, mostly on the four 1h40 home↔school legs. Worth loading with audio case briefs, recorded outline reviews, or a flashcard app rather than losing it entirely.
This is a template, not a cage. Treat the evening/weekend study blocks as the first thing to extend and the "free time" blocks as the first thing to trim when a week gets heavy - never the workout blocks.
