Open Professors & Reading Prep

The semester in phases

Phase 1 - Foundation (Aug 24 - Sep 28)Read, brief, attend, absorb. Build your briefing habit.
Phase 2 - Build (Sep 29 - Oct 31)Start outlining. Midterms hit here. Memo 1 due. Begin career prep.
Phase 3 - Synthesize (Nov 1 - Nov 24)Condense outlines. Memo 2 due. Practice exams. Attack outline mode.
Phase 4 - Finals (Nov 30 - Dec 16)Reading period then exams. Execute. Don’t learn new material.

Pre-semester prep - what to do now

Build reading stamina

Read complex nonfiction for 2+ hours daily without distraction. Law school assigns hundreds of pages of dense text weekly. Your brain needs to be conditioned to retain on a single pass.

Read actual opinions

Use the reading pages in the directory above. Start with Vosburg, Carlill, and Pierson v. Post - short, readable, foundational. Graduate to Palsgraf and Pennoyer next.

Improve typing speed

Your exam grade depends on how much structured analysis you can type in 3 hours. Target 70+ WPM consistently. Use keybr.com or typingclub.com.

Build your calendar system now

Map out your weeks. Block coaching shifts at Brooklyn Boulders, flag exam windows (Dec 2-16), and build in reading blocks. Automation removes decision fatigue during the semester.

Communicate your schedule early

Give your employer your exam dates - Dec 2-16 - as early as possible. Surprises during finals are manageable conflicts you don’t need.

Access BLS resources early

Log into BLSConnect and Canvas. Activate Core Grammar for Lawyers with your professor’s class code. Check the 1L library guide at guides.brooklaw.edu/1l.

BLS-specific and external resources to use

BLSConnect - your central hub for academics, career, pro bono, and student orgs.
Exams-on-File (via BLSConnect) - past exam papers from your professors. Use these to practice issue spotting under time pressure.
BLS Library - open Mon-Fri 9am-5pm and Saturday 12-4pm during the semester. Schedule one-on-one sessions with reference librarians for research help.
Academic Success office - weekly Friday Legal Skills workshops. Go early in the semester, not just before finals.
Career Development Center - start engaging in October. 1L summer internship searches open Dec 1.
Justia - free access to federal and state case law, statutes, and regulations.
CourtListener - an open database of legal opinions and dockets, useful for accessing court records without a paid database.
